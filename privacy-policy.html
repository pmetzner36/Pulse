Pulse Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Here is how we handle your data.

Effective Date: February 6, 2026  |  Developer: Paul Metzner

Summary: Pulse is a mood-tracking and community app. We collect only the data necessary to provide the service. We do not sell your data, we do not run ads, and we do not use third-party analytics SDKs. You can export or delete your data at any time from within the app.

1. Introduction

Pulse ("the App") is developed and operated by Paul Metzner ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the rights you have over your information.

By creating an account or using Pulse, you agree to the collection and use of your data as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Provide the service: Display your mood history, render mood entries on the community map, and enable city chat and Subground forums.
  • City detection: Your GPS coordinates are used to determine your city so you can participate in location-based community features.
  • Map anonymization: When your mood entry appears on the public map, your username is displayed in an anonymized format (first letter followed by "***") to protect your identity.
  • Content moderation: User-generated messages (chat messages and forum posts) are sent to the OpenAI API for automated safety and content moderation before being published.
  • Account management: Your email (if provided) may be used to communicate important service updates or respond to support requests.

4. Third-Party Services

We use a limited number of third-party services to operate Pulse. We do not use any third-party advertising networks or analytics SDKs.

Apple (Sign in with Apple)

Authentication is handled through Apple's Sign in with Apple service. Apple provides us with a unique user identifier and, optionally, your email address. Apple's privacy policy applies to data they process: apple.com/legal/privacy.

OpenAI (Content Moderation)

User-generated messages in city chats and Subground forums are sent to the OpenAI API to check for harmful, abusive, or unsafe content before publication. OpenAI processes these messages solely for moderation purposes. OpenAI's data usage policies apply: openai.com/policies/privacy-policy.

Per OpenAI's API data usage policy, data sent through the API is not used to train OpenAI's models.

Railway (Hosting)

Our backend server and PostgreSQL database are hosted on Railway. Railway acts as a data processor on our behalf. Railway's privacy policy: railway.app/legal/privacy.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted on Railway's infrastructure. We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data, including:

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) between the App and our servers.
  • Secure authentication via Sign in with Apple.
  • Anonymized display of usernames on the public mood map.

While we strive to protect your data, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the service.

  • Account deletion: You can delete your account and all associated data directly from within the App. Upon deletion, we permanently remove your profile, mood submissions, community posts, and any other personal data from our database.
  • Data export: You can export a copy of your data from within the App at any time.
  • Timeframe: Deletion requests are processed promptly. Your data will be removed from active systems within 30 days. Residual copies in encrypted backups may persist for up to 90 days before being overwritten.

7. Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure: Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Right to data portability: Receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Right to restrict processing: Request that we limit how we use your data.
  • Right to object: Object to our processing of your personal data.
  • Right to withdraw consent: Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, use the in-app data export and deletion features or contact us at paul@pulsemood.app. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

The legal basis for processing your data is (a) your consent when creating an account, (b) the performance of a contract (providing the service to you), and (c) our legitimate interest in maintaining a safe community through content moderation.

8. Children's Privacy

Pulse is not designed for or directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, we will take steps to delete that information promptly. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us at paul@pulsemood.app.

9. California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you have the right under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) to:

  • Know what personal data we collect and how it is used.
  • Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Opt out of the sale of personal data. We do not sell your personal data.
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising your privacy rights.

10. International Data Transfers

Our servers are hosted in the United States via Railway. If you access Pulse from outside the United States, your data may be transferred to and processed in the United States. By using the App, you consent to this transfer. We take reasonable steps to ensure your data is treated securely and in accordance with this Privacy Policy.
